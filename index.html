P6 Student FAQ: All Your Questions Answered!

Will I ever be forced to sing solo alone on stage?
No way! Choir is all about team harmony. You will always sing together with your section friends. Solo parts are purely voluntary for students who want to try out!
Can boys join Choir?
Absolutely! Boys bring essential tenor and bass warmth to our choir harmony. We love having boys join our choir family!
Will CCA interfere with my Sec 1 studies and exams?
Our practices are structured twice a week with regular breaks. CCA sessions pause during major examination periods so you can study stress-free.
Do I need prior singing experience or music reading skills?
Not at all! We teach everything from basic vocal warm-ups and breathing to reading musical notation step-by-step.
How do I select Choir during Sec 1 CCA Selection Week?
During your Sec 1 orientation week in January, simply select Choir as your 1st Choice CCA option on the school portal!
