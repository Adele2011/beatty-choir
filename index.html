P6 Student & Parent FAQ

Do I need prior singing or music reading experience?
Not at all! We welcome all interested students regardless of background. Our friendly instructor and senior student leaders will guide you step-by-step in vocal technique and reading notation.
How does choir practice fit with Sec 1 academic studies?
Our practices take place twice a week with fixed, manageable schedules, giving you plenty of time to focus on your studies and enjoy your secondary school life.
How do I join Choir during Sec 1 CCA Selection Week?
During your Sec 1 orientation week in January, simply select Choir as your 1st Choice CCA option on the school portal!
